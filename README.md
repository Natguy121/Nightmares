# Rooftop Mimic 3D

A 3D third-person parkour game on one small rooftop level with a finish flag.
Run, jump and slide across the rooftops to the flag in all 5 rounds without the
Mimic catching you, not once.

## Controls

**Phone (Roblox-style):** drag on the left side of the screen to move (a joystick
appears under your thumb), drag on the right side to look around, and tap the
**JUMP** and **SLIDE** buttons.

**Computer:** WASD / arrows to move, drag the mouse (or Q/E) to look, Space to jump,
Shift to slide.

Sliding is faster than running and fits under the yellow beams.

## The Mimic hides everywhere

Chimneys, vents, water tanks, billboards, dumpsters, sheds. Red eyes blink out of random
hiding spots, real or fake. When you get close to the one it's really in, the spot shakes
and it bursts out and chases you. Outrun it (or outlast it) and it slips into another
hiding spot further ahead.

## It learns

The level splits twice (high stepping stones vs. the walkway with beams; narrow beams
vs. pillars). The Mimic remembers which route you take, where you stand still, and
where it caught you, and hides there in later rounds. It hides in more places every
round. Its memory is saved in your browser until you press "Make it forget me".

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser (it loads Three.js from cdnjs, so it needs an internet
connection). To play on a phone, host it with GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy
from branch) and open the link.
